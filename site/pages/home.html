The work comes back to you

Watch a card. Read the change, or choose to get it by email.

Watching is one person's interest in one piece of work. The bell collects what happens next — a move, a block, an edit, a note, a due date — with the board and column, and a row opens the card where it lives.

The inbox

Only what changed, from now

A watch starts when you make it. The card's earlier history stays in its Activity; the inbox shows only what happens next. The creator, each assignee and a note's author watch without asking, and a Viewer can watch too.

  • Moved, blocked, edited, noted, filed, due-date and health changes
  • Newest first, with the board and the column
  • Mark all read; a deleted item drops out

The digest

Plain text, when you choose

Turn on email in your profile for an immediate, daily or weekly message. One message per period, written at send time: watched changes, what was assigned, where you were mentioned, what is due and the watched initiatives at risk.

  • Your time zone decides the morning
  • No tracking pixel, no third-party mail service
  • Absent, not broken, when no relay is configured

Private by default

Your board stays on your own server

Nothing leaves the machine unless you turn on webhooks or mail.

Your work stays yours

Changes save as you make them, or as soon as the connection returns, and land in the order they were made, so everybody reads the same copy.

FAQ

Straightforward answers

Do I need an account to try it?

No. The demo is a complete board with example work already in it. Accounts are optional and only matter when you run your own copy.

What if two of us edit the same card?

The person editing holds the card while they work, so the other sees it is held. A change based on an older copy is refused, and you are shown the newer one.

Will it email me about changes?

Only if you ask. Watch a card and the in-app bell collects what happens next; email is opt-in in your profile, immediate, daily or weekly, and plain text. An instance with no mail relay simply does not offer it.

Do you train AI on our work?

No. Nothing is collected or used to train anything, and your work never leaves your copy.

How do we run it ourselves and keep it safe?

One command with Docker, or three with Node. Everything is kept in one SQLite file you can copy or download whenever you like. See how to run it.
