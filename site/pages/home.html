FAQ

Straightforward answers

Do I need an account to try it?

No. The demo is a complete board with example work already in it. Accounts are optional and only matter when you run your own copy.

What happens when I'm offline?

Changes are saved as you make them, even without a connection. When you're back online, they're sent in the order you made them.

Do you train AI on our work?

No. Nothing is collected and nothing scrapes your work. Your notes, names and files never leave the place you put them.

How do we run it ourselves and keep it safe?

It comes with simple instructions for running it on your own machine or server. Everything is kept in one file you can copy or download whenever you like. See how to run it.
