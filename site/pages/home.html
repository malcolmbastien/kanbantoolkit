A board for teams

Work is a graph.
Boards are views over it.

A card is not a row in a list. It is an item with a parent, an owner, a history and a place — and a board is one way of looking at those. Move the work, and everything that rolls up from it moves with it.

Start free See what it does

What it does

  • Rolls up. An initiative's status is its work, not a field somebody sets. Split a column and the cards in it do not move; the board is a view, and the model does not change shape to accommodate it.
  • Keeps the record. Every move, edit and refusal is part of the card's own history, with the name of the person who did it — including after they leave.
  • Stays out of the way. One board is one workspace, and a workspace is the people you invited. No account is needed to read this page, and none is needed to try the demo.
