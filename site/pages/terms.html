Terms

The terms of using Flowline, in plain words. Task 150 publishes the reviewed text; this page is where it will live.

Your workspace, your work

You own the work you put into a workspace. We keep it, run it, and delete it when you ask. A workspace is isolated: what happens in one is invisible to another.

What we ask

Invite the people you mean to. Do not use the service to attack it or anybody else.

Ending it

A workspace administrator can delete the workspace at any time. Closing it deletes its accounts too; export first if you may want it again.
