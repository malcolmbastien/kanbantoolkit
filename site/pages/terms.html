Terms

The terms of using Flowline, in plain words. They are short because the product is small and the promises are meant.

Your workspace, your work

You own what you put into a workspace. We keep it, run it, and hand it back — as a file, from the board's own menu — whenever you ask. We do not read it to train anything, and we do not sell it.

A workspace is isolated from every other workspace: what happens in one is invisible to another, and no member of one can reach into another.

What we ask of you

  • Invite the people you mean to invite, and give them the role they need and no more.
  • Do not use the service to attack it, to probe it without telling us, or to break the law with it.
  • Keep your own copy of anything you could not bear to lose. Export is one click, and it is there for this reason.

The service

The demo instance is run carefully and aims to stay up, but no particular uptime is promised, and there is no status page claiming otherwise. Software you install yourself is yours to keep running, and its uptime is your machine's.

Ending it

A workspace administrator can delete the workspace at any time; its accounts go with it. There is no way back except a new workspace and a new account, so export first if you may want it again. We may close an account or a workspace that is being used to harm somebody else, and we will say why.

Questions

support@flowline.example, answered within two working days. The privacy notice says what we hold and who processes it.
