Changelog

What changed, newest first, in the words a person would use. A date is a day the change was live, not the day somebody thought about it.

24 October 2026

  • The site has a front door. The app now lives at /app, and this is the page it opens from. Old links still work: they land on the same place under /app.
  • A workspace can be deleted by its administrator, and only by them. Its accounts go with it; its work does not linger anywhere else.
  • Removing a member ends their account. The work they did stays, and every place that names them reads “Former member”.
  • One deployment, one team. The workspace comes with the installation and is named by it; the first account to sign in administers it. There is no sign-up page and no second workspace.
  • Flowline is free software, under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 or later: install it, change it, run it.
  • Invitations are links that can be revoked, and a link opened after the app moved to /app lands on the app rather than the site.

22 October 2026

  • Sessions can be listed and ended one by one, or everywhere at once, from a profile.
  • Groups grant a role once to many people, and can be turned off without deleting them.
  • Live — a change on one device arrives on another without a reload, over a socket where the host has one and by polling where it does not.

21 October 2026

  • Roles are decided by one matrix on the server, read on every request: a change takes effect on the next one, with no re-login.
  • The workspace lives on the server, and the browser holds a copy that catches up.
