Help

The short answers, here rather than in an inbox. The longer list is task 158; these are the three things people ask for first.

Getting in

Sign in with the account you already have — Google, Microsoft, or an identity provider your operator configured — or start free and we will help you make a workspace. An invitation arrives as a link; it works once, and you can be sent another.

Taking a workspace with you

Open the board, choose More → Export in the workspace toolbar, and the file lands in your downloads. It is the workspace: boards, lanes, cards, placements, the history and all. Nothing is left behind, because nothing is generated — the file is the data.

Closing a workspace

Admin → Workspace account → Delete this workspace. The workspace's administrator is the only person who can do it, the workspace's name is typed to confirm, and the accounts that belong to it are deleted with it. Export first if you may want it again: there is no undo.

Reaching a person

support@flowline.example — answered within two working days. Security reports go to security@flowline.example, and are answered faster.
