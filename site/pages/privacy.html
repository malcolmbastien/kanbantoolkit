Privacy notice

What we hold, who sees it, how long it stays, and how to take it with you or have it removed. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts: the only things that see your work are the services listed below, and each one is named.

What we hold

  • Your account. A name, an email address, and the identity your provider verified when you signed in. The address is the one the provider vouched for; Flowline does not let anybody type a different one.
  • Your workspace. Its boards, lanes, cards, placements, objectives and the words you put in them — the work itself.
  • What happened. An audit trail of access changes — who invited whom, who changed a role, who signed in — kept for 90 days. It records field names, not the values of the work.
  • Sessions. Stored as hashes, with the browser that opened them and when each was last used. An address is stored only as a salted hash, never as an address.

That is the whole of it. There is no billing record, no payment provider and no card: the software is free, and an instance holds one team's work and nothing commercial beside it.

How long it stays

A workspace stays until its administrator deletes it. Sessions end after twelve hours, or thirty days when a browser asked to be remembered; the audit trail is kept for 90 days and then pruned. Error reports are pruned with it.

Taking it with you

Export a workspace from the board's own menu: the file is the workspace — boards, lanes, cards, history and all — and it opens anywhere the app runs. It is the same file the backup runbook restores from.

Having it deleted

Two different promises, and both are kept:

  • A person is removed by a workspace administrator. Their account, their ways in and their sessions are deleted; the work they did stays, and every place that names them reads “Former member”.
  • A workspace is deleted by its administrator, and its accounts are deleted with it. That is the end of it: to come back, somebody makes a new workspace and a new account.

If something goes wrong

Write to privacy@flowline.example. We answer within two working days. A security problem goes to security@flowline.example instead, and is answered faster.

Changes
