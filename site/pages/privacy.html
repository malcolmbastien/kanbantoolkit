Privacy notice

What we hold, who processes it, and how to take it away. The complete notice — including the processor list checked against the deployment's own configuration — is task 150.

What we hold

Your account (name, email, the identity a provider verified), the workspace you are in, and the work in it. Sessions are stored as hashes; addresses are stored hashed.

Who processes it

The identity providers you sign in with, the host this runs on, and the error log — and nothing else.

Taking it with you

A workspace exports itself at any time. Deleting a workspace deletes its accounts with it; the work of a removed member stays, attributed to a former member.
