Security

Sessions are hashed at rest and revoked in one batch; roles are decided by one matrix on the server, read on every request; a workspace is a scope, not a filter. The full trust set — DPA, sub-processors, refunds — is task 157.

Report a vulnerability

security@flowline.example. Tell us what you did, what happened, and how to reproduce it. We will not take legal action against good-faith research, and we will tell you when it is fixed.
